# Online Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends using Power BI. The goal is to identify key business insights and provide recommendations to improve revenue and profitability.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data, but identifying profitable products, customer segments, and regions can be challenging. This project helps stakeholders understand performance trends and make data-driven decisions.

## Dataset Information

Dataset: Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains:
- Orders
- Sales
- Profit
- Customer Segments
- Product Categories
- Regions
- Discounts
- Shipping Information

## Key Insights

- Sales showed an overall upward trend from 2014 to 2017.
- The West region generated the highest sales.
- Technology was the most profitable category.
- Consumer customers contributed the highest revenue.
- Profitability was concentrated in Technology and Office Supplies.

## Recommendations

- Improve profitability in the Furniture category.
- Focus marketing efforts on the Consumer segment.
- Prioritize inventory for top-selling products.
- Expand successful regional strategies to lower-performing regions.
- Continue investing in high-performing product categories.
